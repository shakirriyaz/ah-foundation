Featured Impact Story

Faith in Action: Serving Communities in Jamaica

Working alongside Fresh Bread Ministries, Refuge of Hope, and dedicated ministry partners, the AH Foundation helped support outreach efforts throughout Montego Bay and the Westmoreland region of Jamaica.

Impact highlights include:

  • 150+ community members served
  • Health screenings and basic medical support
  • Children's ministry and family engagement
  • Prayer, encouragement, and faith-centered outreach

This is what meaningful partnership looks like: organizations coming together to serve people and strengthen communities.
