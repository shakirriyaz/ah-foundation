Why We Exist

Business Success. Community Impact.

The AH Foundation was established from a belief that success creates both an opportunity and a responsibility to give back.

As INTEGRITY grows and serves customers in communities across the country, the AH Foundation seeks opportunities to invest back into those communities by supporting organizations already making a meaningful difference.

Rather than simply providing funding, the Foundation seeks to develop meaningful relationships with organizations that understand their communities and are creating lasting impact.

The Values Behind Every Partnership

Four commitments shape how the AH Foundation identifies, builds, and sustains relationships with the organizations we support.

01

Faith

Supporting organizations whose work reflects compassion, service, and Christian values.

02

Community

Investing in organizations addressing meaningful needs within the communities they serve.

03

Partnership

Building relationships with organizations rather than simply providing financial support.

04

Impact

Measuring success by lasting change in the lives of individuals, families, and communities.
